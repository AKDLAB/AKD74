About the Group

The MMST Group at the Institute of Nano Science and Technology, Mohali, develops multifunctional nanomaterials and sensing platforms for sustainable technologies. Our work spans biosensors and chemosensors, nanomaterial-based energy applications, wastewater remediation, and the recovery of valuable resources from electronic and other low-grade waste streams — combining materials design with real-world environmental and energy challenges.
