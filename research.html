Research

Our group works at the interface of materials science, sensing, and sustainability — designing multifunctional nanomaterials that address real environmental and energy challenges.

🧪

Bio and Chemo Sensors

Development of sensitive and selective sensing platforms for biological and chemical species, combining nanomaterials with transduction techniques for rapid, reliable detection.

🌍

Sensors for Environmental Analytes

Design of sensor systems targeted at environmentally important analytes, supporting monitoring of pollutants and contaminants relevant to environmental and public health.

⚡

Energy Applications of Nanomaterials

Exploration of nanomaterials and their composites for energy-related applications, including materials engineered for improved performance in energy devices.

♻

Recovery from E-waste & Low-grade Sources

Development of approaches to recover valuable products from electronic waste and other low-grade sources, supporting resource circularity and sustainable materials use.
